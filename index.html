Parag Bhatnagar

I'm interested in how intelligent automation and AI can improve healthcare, particularly how these technologies can be deployed responsibly and at scale.

My research focuses on robotic process automation, agentic AI, digital transformation, and healthcare systems.

Currently, I work at Health New Zealand, helping develop and scale automation across the public health system.

Previously, I worked in business intelligence and health informatics at the University of Auckland and Auckland District Health Board. I also spent time at the University of Cambridge as a Visiting Fellow, exploring digital transformation and emerging technologies.

I publish and present research on healthcare automation, AI, and crisis management.
