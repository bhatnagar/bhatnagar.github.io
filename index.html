Parag Bhatnagar

I'm focused on two problems in healthcare: implementing AI and automation safely, and ensuring they don't become another layer of cost.

My work and research explore how intelligent automation can improve public services while remaining accountable, practical, and financially sustainable.

Currently, I work at Health New Zealand , helping develop and scale automation across the public health system.

Previously, I worked in business intelligence and health informatics at the University of Auckland and Auckland District Health Board . I also spent time at the University of Cambridge as a Visiting Fellow, undertaking research associated with the ESRC Digital Good Network .
